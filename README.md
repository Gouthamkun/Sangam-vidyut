# Sangam Vidyut

Sangam Vidyut is a research-grade renewable-energy adoption simulation framework designed to model how households adopt renewable and solar energy.

The framework simulates the adoption process under the influence of:
1. Household demographic characteristics
2. Historical solar adoption patterns
3. Solar panel pricing and technology costs
4. Household social-network influence
5. Government subsidies and policy parameters

## Core Entities
The system simulates interactions between:
- **Consumer Agents:** Households deciding on solar adoption based on economic and social factors.
- **Government Agents:** Entities dictating subsidy structures and policy changes.
- **Industry Agents:** Entities representing the supply side, dictating costs based on technology trends.
