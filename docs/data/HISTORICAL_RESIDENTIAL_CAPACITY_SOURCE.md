# Historical Residential Capacity Source (Phase 4D.6)

## 1. Context & Motivation
Phase 4D.5B directed the search for an official historical (pre-2024) **residential rooftop installed capacity (MW)** dataset to act as Target P0-B. 

## 2. Source Candidates Investigated
- **Rajya Sabha Starred Question No. 210** (08.08.2023, "Targets for rooftop solar programme")
- **Lok Sabha UQ 3579** (10.08.2023, "Residential Rooftop Solar Systems")

## 3. Verification Outcome
Despite confirming the general existence and subject matter of these parliamentary questions via index summaries, the **original official PDF documents** could not be directly retrieved or verified (due to parliamentary archive access restrictions and lack of direct PDF mirrors).

Without access to the primary PDFs, it is impossible to rigorously verify:
- The exact table titles and column semantics.
- Whether the capacity is cumulative or incremental.
- Whether it strictly separates residential vs. overall rooftop.
- The precise state/UT coverage.

## 4. Conclusion
Because the methodological constraints forbid assuming metric semantics from secondary search snippets, and require direct verification from the original official document:

**NO VERIFIED HISTORICAL RESIDENTIAL CAPACITY SOURCE**

## 5. Capacity Model Gap
Because a historical residential capacity target could not be verified, the capacity model (kW/household) lacks a direct empirical MW target to validate against for the pre-2024 period. Future empirical data (such as official state/DISCOM records or National Portal statistics) will be required to define the `system_size_distribution_kw`.
